# WORKY / ENGLISH VERTICAL FILM

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** When something breaks, a nearby professional is easier to find with Worky.
- **Identity:** Worky's navy, orange, gold, and cream; Space Grotesk display with DM Sans support. The supplied wordmark stays untouched.
- **Hero:** A runaway waterline that becomes a route to a local professional, then resolves into the Worky mark.
- **Motion:** Escalating type pops; a waterline-to-search-path match cut; rotating profile cards that lock into a repair check.
- **Arc:** Domestic chaos snaps into purposeful discovery, then relief. The tempo stays playful, never frantic enough to obscure the message.
- **Payoff:** The final orange location pin resolves into the supplied logo on a navy field, visually connecting the repair to the city's network.
- **Format:** 9:16 portrait.

## Shot script

Short, silent-readable English copy. No narration: the team will record its own voiceover separately.

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.00s / 4.00s — “PIPE DOWN?”
**Purpose:** Hook with a recognizable household emergency, then point toward a solution.

**Exact content:** At 0.08s: “OH NO.” at 28vmin, cropped deliberately but kept legible. At 0.52s: second pop “LEAK!” at 34vmin, in orange. At 1.16s: “Pipe burst?” at 8vmin, navy. At 2.05s: “Don’t panic.” at 4vmin, navy. All copy remains until the outgoing move.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Cream background. A huge abstract navy pipe elbow occupies lower-center, its opening a circular focal point; it is deliberately graphic rather than photoreal. Gold droplets and an orange water ribbon fly upward around it. Type owns the upper half; the pipe and water occupy lower half. Strong navy shadow shapes make the pipe feel like a cut-paper prop.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00s pipe elbow pops in with stiff overshoot; a small gold droplet is already visible. 0.45s “OH NO.” snaps in. 0.92s pipe joint kicks and releases a fan of three droplets. 1.18s “LEAK!” punches in; orange waterline whips upward behind letters without crossing their counters. 1.95s “Pipe burst?” replaces the first alarm with a stable phrase. 2.65s droplets visibly reverse and funnel toward the pipe joint. 3.30s that joint expands as a dark circular lens and takes over the frame; no empty tail.

**Transition contract:** The growing circular pipe joint becomes the location lens at the exact cut. Orange waterline continues as the map route on the next side; compound match cut combines aperture expansion and orange path continuity.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG pipe, splash, waterline, typography. Brand palette and supplied logo reserved for final shot. No generated imagery.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental nu-disco bed, no vocals. Opener changes at sub-second intervals. Cut aimed at a generated music accent; actual onset alignment to be measured.
