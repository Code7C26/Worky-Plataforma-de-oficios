---
name: Selective mobile motion
description: Worky's approved mobile motion direction for future interaction polish.
---

For Worky mobile, use brief motion selectively for main-tab changes, prominent actions, entry into detail routes, and success confirmations. Keep secondary and cancel controls simple, and respect the device's reduced-motion preference.

**Why:** The product owner chose a small set of useful interaction points instead of animating every touch.

**How to apply:** When extending mobile interaction feedback, prioritize the approved high-value actions and route transitions; avoid adding motion to secondary controls by default.
