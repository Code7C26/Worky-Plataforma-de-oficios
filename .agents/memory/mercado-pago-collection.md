---
name: Mercado Pago collection
description: Worky's payment lifecycle, nonpayment ranking rule, and separation of development and production credentials.
---

For Worky, the professional first marks a job as performed. Mercado Pago payment finalizes it only after the provider confirms approval; cash finalizes only after both client and professional confirm receipt. Reviews remain unavailable until finalization. The client explicitly starts Mercado Pago checkout, and Worky's account receives the payment; do not imply automatic transfers to professionals.

Visit fees are separate from job payments: the Partner sets the fee, the client pays when accepting the visit, and Worky keeps 15% while 85% is held in the Partner's Worky balance. Release early when the client confirms, or automatically after 24 hours from Partner completion if no problem is reported. A problem report pauses the balance for admin review. For cash visits, the Partner collects directly and Worky records the 15% as a debt against future available balance. A Partner can report a client no-show only after recording arrival and waiting 10 minutes; either party can report other visit problems. Refunds are not automatic; a Mercado Pago refund must be processed with the provider and confirmed by its webhook.

Repeated failed payment attempts may lower a professional's discovery ranking only after two distinct Mercado Pago checkouts have remained unpaid for over 24 hours within 30 days. Count only real checkout preferences; API/provider failures and cash jobs are not nonpayments. The broader future ranking stays dormant until the first real, confirmed job payment in production; then confirmed visit-commission debt and at least two administrator-reviewed activity signals in 30 days may modestly lower visibility. Chat or cancellation alone is never proof, and dismissed or reversed signals do not count.

**Why:** The product owners chose payment after the work is performed, both Mercado Pago and cash, and explicitly selected the two-in-30-days threshold. They also asked to keep future ranking changes inactive until real payments exist, with an automatic production-only activation gate and human review for activity signals. For visits they chose client payment at acceptance, 15/85 allocation, early client confirmation plus a 24-hour no-report release, a review hold for disputes, and cash commission offsets. Separating job and visit ledgers avoids accidentally making one policy govern the other.

**How to apply:** Keep development and production Access Tokens and webhook signing secrets separate. For Mercado Pago Checkout Pro with Orders, a test Access Token can also start with `APP_USR`; do not infer its environment from the prefix. In development, return only Mercado Pago's sandbox checkout URL. Treat signed notifications plus a provider-side payment lookup as the source of payment status; redirects alone never confirm payment. Keep the nonpayment ranking modest and based only on the agreed rolling time window, and never let test, sandbox, pending, rejected, or refunded payments open the future gate. Never release a Mercado Pago visit balance from an admin report unless the visit was marked complete; provider refunds require their own provider confirmation.

Cash job commissions are separate from visit fees. Worky charges 15% of the final job amount only after the client and Partner both confirm a cash payment. Record the commission once, offset it against future available Worky balance, and keep any unpaid remainder as a positive amount due. Do not apply this job commission to Mercado Pago jobs or count already-offset amounts again.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly chose a 15% commission for cash-paid jobs, settled against future Worky balance, and asked to include only amounts that have not already been paid. The existing 15% visit fee remains a separate policy.

**How to apply:** Create a distinct, idempotent job-commission record after mutual cash-payment confirmation; keep visit and job commission totals distinguishable, enforce Partner-only access, and do not add a separate manual checkout unless the user requests it.
